## 📄 Project Overview<br><br>

This project analyzes a large dataset related to New York City’s 🗽 bus transportation system to identify patterns and factors contributing to bus delays and breakdowns. The goal is to improve the efficiency and reliability of the city’s public transportation by drawing actionable insights from the data.<br><br>

## 🚦 Problem Statement<br>

The New York Division of Transportation is experiencing frequent bus breakdowns and delays, causing:<br>
	•	❌ Inconvenience for commuters<br>
	•	📉 Strain on public transportation resources<br><br>

## Questions Addressed:<br>

1.	🛑 What are the most common reasons for delays and breakdowns?<br>
2.	⏱️ How do delay times vary by bus company and borough?<br>
3.	📅 Is there a correlation between specific days of the week and the frequency of breakdowns or delays?<br><br>

## 🔎 Data Analysis Process<br>

### 📊 Step 1: Data Aggregation<br>

•	The dataset contains detailed information about bus delays and breakdowns categorized by:<br>
•	🚨 Reason (e.g., mechanical problems, heavy traffic, weather conditions).<br>
•	⚙️ Type of Issue (Breakdowns vs. Running Late).<br><br>

### 📈 Step 2: Data Visualization<br><br>

•	A pivot table summarized the data by breakdown and delay reasons.<br>
•	A bar chart visually represented the distribution of issues.<br>

### 💡 Step 3: Insights and Findings<br>

## 🛠️ Breakdowns<br>

•	The leading cause of breakdowns is mechanical problems ⚙️, followed by buses that “Won’t Start” 🛑.<br>
•	🔧 Recommendation: Increase preventive maintenance frequency (e.g., monthly or quarterly servicing).<br>
<br>
## 🚦 Delays<br>

•	The primary cause of delays is heavy traffic 🚗🚕🚙.<br>
•	🕒 Recommendation: Optimize student pickup/drop-off times to avoid high-traffic hours.<br><br>


 ## ✅ Results<br>

Key Takeaways:<br>

1.	🛠️ Mechanical Problems are the major cause of breakdowns, necessitating better maintenance schedules.<br>
2.	🚦 Heavy Traffic leads most delays, suggesting the need for route and timing optimization.<br>
3.	📊 Borough-specific patterns and day-of-week analysis could refine strategies further.<br><br>

## 🚀 Potential Solutions:<br>

•	🔍 Predictive Maintenance Program: Use historical data to anticipate issues.<br>
•	🛣️ Traffic-Aware Route Planning: Integrate real-time traffic data.<br>
•	📅 Time-of-Day Adjustments: Focus pickups/drop-offs on non-peak hours.<br><br>
